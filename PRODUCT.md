# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose

5n7.me is Shunta Komatsu's personal portfolio. It presents his work history,
education, writing, talks, research, awards, interests, and contact details.

## Constraints

- The owner requested a complete redesign and delegated design and stack choices.
- Preserve the existing factual content, descriptions, dates, titles, and links.
- Use `src/data/portfolio.ts` as the content source. Preserve the introductory
  paragraph in `src/pages/index.astro` as well.
- Do not invent projects, results, endorsements, or availability claims.
- Keep the English page and the original Japanese publication and talk titles.
- Retain static hosting at `https://5n7.me`, page metadata, theme selection,
  keyboard access, and mobile and print layouts.

## Evidence

The repository contains seven employment records, two degrees, seven talks,
one academic paper, six articles, four awards, and four contact links. The
existing profile image is a corgi illustration, not a photograph of the owner.

## Design intent

The owner wants the work and factual record to establish credibility. The
presentation should be restrained and easy to read. Audience assumption for
this redesign: peers and prospective collaborators reviewing his experience.
